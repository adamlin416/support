FAQ / 常見問題

My App is crashing or freezing. App 出現閃退或當機。

Please insure your iOS and App match the latest version available. Restarting your device often helps. 請確認您的 iOS 和 App 皆為最新版本。重新啟動裝置通常能解決問題。

Widget is not displaying data? 桌面小工具沒有顯示數據？

Try removing and re-adding the widget. If it remains blank, open the Main App once to refresh the data connection. 嘗試移除並重新加入小工具。若是空白，請開啟一次 App 以更新數據連結。

How is my data stored? 我的數據如何儲存？

Your data is stored locally on your device and synced via iCloud (if enabled). We do not collect personal data on our servers. 您的數據儲存在本機並透過 iCloud 同步（若開啟）。我們不會將個人數據上傳至伺服器。
